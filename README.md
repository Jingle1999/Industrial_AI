# Decision Corpus

> **The Industrial Decision Corpus**
>
> An open knowledge base and reference architecture for Decision Intelligence,
> Industrial AI and Autonomous Operations.

---

## Vision

Industrial AI is not fundamentally about artificial intelligence.

It is about making better decisions.

Every factory, every production line, every supply chain and every engineering organization consists of millions of interconnected decisions.

The mission of this repository is to build an open, structured and reusable knowledge base that captures these decisions, their context and their outcomes.

The long-term objective is to create the **Industrial Decision Corpus**—a reference dataset for Decision Intelligence, autonomous industrial systems and the next generation of Enterprise Decision Layers.

---

## Core Principles

We believe that autonomous operations are built upon five capabilities:

1. Capture Decisions
2. Understand Decisions
3. Standardize Decisions
4. Simulate Decisions
5. Execute Decisions

Every documented decision contributes to making future decisions better.

## Decision Case

Every industrial decision can be represented as:

```
State
    ↓
Decision
    ↓
Outcome
    ↓
Lessons Learned
    ↓
Decision Pattern
```

Over time, thousands of decision cases form reusable decision patterns.

Decision patterns form decision models.

Decision models enable autonomous decision agents.

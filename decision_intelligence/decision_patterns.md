# Decision Patterns

Decision patterns are reusable abstractions derived from successful decision cases.

---

## Pattern 001

Name:
Simulate Before Commit

Context:
High uncertainty
High consequence of failure

Decision:
Perform simulation before execution

Expected Outcome:
Risk reduction

Sources:
NASA
Aerospace
Industrial Engineering

---

## Pattern 002

Name:
Human Approval Before Escalation

Context:
Safety critical environment

Decision:
Require approval before execution

Expected Outcome:
Reduced operational risk

Sources:
Manufacturing
Aviation
Healthcare

---

## Pattern 003

Name:
Hold Before Act

Context:
Incomplete evidence
Uncertain system state

Decision:
Pause execution until evidence is sufficient

Expected Outcome:
Reduced unintended consequences

Sources:
Industrial Safety
Nuclear Operations
Air Traffic Control
virtauto Governance

## Pattern 004

Name:
Safety Before Performance

Context:
Conflict between safety and productivity

Decision:
Prioritize safety constraints

Expected Outcome:
Avoid catastrophic failure

Sources:
Aviation
Automotive
Chemical Industry
Healthcare

## Pattern 005

Name:
Detect Early, Act Early

Context:
Weak signals indicate future problems

Decision:
Intervene before failure occurs

Expected Outcome:
Reduced downtime and recovery cost

Sources:
Predictive Maintenance
Toyota Production System
Reliability Engineering

## Pattern 006

Name:
Local Optimization Can Harm Global Optimization

Context:
Subsystem optimization degrades overall system performance

Decision:
Optimize at system level

Expected Outcome:
Improved overall outcome

Sources:
Operations Research
Supply Chain Management
Systems Engineering

# Decision Intelligence

This section explores a fundamental question:

How can successful decisions be identified, reused, and scaled?

The goal is not to predict outcomes from data alone.

The goal is to discover repeatable decision patterns that have demonstrated successful outcomes across domains such as manufacturing, aerospace, logistics, management, and operations.

Core hypothesis:

Situation
→ Decision
→ Outcome
→ Lesson
→ Pattern

If patterns can be identified and formalized, they may become reusable building blocks for future decision-making.

This repository investigates:

- Decision Cases
- Decision Patterns
- Decision Memory
- Decision Graphs
- Authority Graphs
- Governed Autonomy

Related concepts:

- Operations Research
- Decision Intelligence
- Knowledge Graphs
- Cognitive Digital Twins
- Industrial AI

# Decision Cases

A decision case captures:

- Situation
- Constraints
- Decision
- Outcome
- Lessons Learned

---

## NASA Case 001

Source:
NASA Lessons Learned Database

Domain:
Space Exploration

Situation:
High uncertainty during mission development.

Constraints:
- High mission cost
- No recovery after launch
- Safety critical environment

Decision:
Extensive simulation before deployment.

Outcome:
Reduced operational risk.

Lesson Learned:
Simulation is preferable when failure costs are high.

Potential Pattern:
Simulate Before Commit

Decision Category:
Risk Management

Confidence:
High

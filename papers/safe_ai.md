## Explainable AI: A Neurally-Inspired Decision Stack Framework

Authors: J.L. Olds, M.S. Khan, M. Nayebpour, N. Koizumi

Year: 2019

arXiv:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.10300

PDF:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.10300.pdf

### Key Ideas

- Explainable AI (XAI)
- Decision Stack Framework
- Traceable reasoning
- Decision accountability
- Transparent decision processes

### Summary

The paper proposes a Decision Stack Framework inspired by
biological memory systems.

Rather than treating AI decisions as black-box outputs,
the framework models decisions as a sequence of reasoning
layers that can be inspected and explained after execution.

The authors argue that explainability is essential for
trust, accountability, and safe deployment of AI systems.

### Industrial AI Relevance

Industrial AI systems frequently operate in environments
where safety, compliance, and auditability are critical.

Operators and engineers must understand not only what
decision was made, but why it was made.

The Decision Stack concept supports auditable decision
processes that can be inspected after execution.

### Industrial AI Lens

✔ Trust requires transparency

✔ Decisions require traceability

✔ Explainability improves accountability

✔ Safe autonomy requires auditable reasoning

### Relation to Safe AI

As systems gain greater operational authority,
their decision processes must become increasingly
observable and explainable.

The framework reinforces the principle that safe
autonomy requires both technical safeguards and
decision transparency.

Observation:

The framework introduces structured explainability,
but does not address decision contracts,
authority delegation, escalation mechanisms,
or governed execution.
